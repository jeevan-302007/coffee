Espresso

Nature: Born in Italy, poured fast and dark — like a secret told once and never repeated.

Taste: Sharp, bitter, unapologetic, crowned with a thin gold crema that catches the morning light.

Vibe: A midnight drive with the windows down — brief, electric, unforgettable.

Speciality: Small enough to fit in your palm, strong enough to start every love story after it.

Cappuccino

Nature: An Italian classic, foam and milk folded into espresso like lace laid over silk.

Taste: Creamy and soft-spoken, sweet at the edges — a whisper instead of a shout.

Vibe: Sunday mornings in a velvet chair, sunlight through lace curtains.

Speciality: A dusting of cocoa on top, like powder on an old vanity mirror.

Latte

Nature: Espresso drowned gently in steamed milk, a thin foam laid over it like a veil.

Taste: Mild and milky, easy to fall for, easy to stay.

Vibe: Slow jazz on vinyl, a cigarette that never quite gets smoked.

Speciality: Dressed up in vanilla or caramel, always a little glamorous.

Americano

Nature: Espresso stretched thin with hot water, born from soldiers craving something close to home.

Taste: Honest and unhurried, softer than its espresso mother.

Vibe: Black coffee at a chrome diner counter, three in the morning, no one else around.

Speciality: You decide how strong the story gets — just add water.

Macchiato

Nature: Espresso "stained" with the faintest touch of foam, Italian and unbothered.

Taste: Bold and dark, only a whisper of sweetness allowed to interrupt.

Vibe: Red lipstick and a quick exit — no time for goodbyes.

Speciality: Barely any milk, all attitude.

Mocha

Nature: Espresso, chocolate, and steamed milk — dessert wearing a coffee's coat.

Taste: Sweet and rich, indulgent, like a secret kept just for yourself.

Vibe: Old Hollywood glamour, velvet booths, a slow dance in a smoky room.

Speciality: Whipped cream on top, chocolate drizzled like a love letter.

Flat White

Nature: From Australia and New Zealand, espresso wrapped in silky micro-foam.

Taste: Velvet on the tongue, strong enough to remember.

Vibe: Sunglasses indoors, a little too cool, a little too sure of itself.

Speciality: Smaller than a latte, but never small in presence.

Cold Brew

Nature: Steeped slow in cold water for a day, patience turned into flavor.

Taste: Smooth and naturally sweet, nothing bitter left to forgive.

Vibe: Summertime sadness in a mason jar, ice clinking like a lullaby.

Speciality: Keeps for days — some things, thankfully, don't fade fast.

Affogato

Nature: An Italian dessert where hot espresso meets cold vanilla gelato.

Taste: Sweet and creamy, a little bittersweet — hot and cold colliding like old love.

Vibe: The last dance of the evening, dessert and drink poured into one glass.

Speciality: Half indulgence, half farewell.
